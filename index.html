About SUBSTRATE PRO

A Vertascan conceptual AEC authoring environment, combining precision 2D linework with coordinated, parameter-driven 3D modeling. The modular engine offers intelligent wall chains, snapping, editable building elements, hosted opening families, grid/level tools, structural framing, facade and roof studies, assemblies and discipline-based drafting.

Documentation: Model-linked annotations, construction-sheet layouts, symbol and material hatch libraries, room and component schedules, orthographic reviews, SVG export, and preliminary IFC interoperability.

Implementation status: A development-stage BIM/CAD application. Building-code presets and preliminary connection/sheet outputs are not professional certification, engineered approval, or guaranteed IFC round-trip interoperability.

Guided BIM tutorial

Each action below opens the relevant existing workspace tool. The tutorial does not create substitute geometry or bypass BIM data transactions.

01 · Start with a project
Open a clean BIM document or inspect the prebuilt example.

02 · Draft connected walls
Switch to Plan, choose Architecture → Wall. Use grid/endpoint snap; choose Wall Polyline for multi-segment walls.

03 · Inspect 3D geometry
Switch to Iso and navigate the shared model. Edit thickness, height or material through Properties.

04 · Annotate and plot
Use the drafting LOD options, intelligent dimensions, family symbols, schedules, and Construction Sheet Studio.

05 · Review additional commands
Explore levels, roof/facade generators, grids, assembly catalog, room schedules and discipline layers in the workspace.
